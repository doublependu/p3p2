    This page stands in for a game with a menu of its own: it uses the client without its panel. The ball's flight is where and when it left, and how fast, on the room's clock: every page draws the same ball from it. The page of the player the ball is heading to decides whether it was returned, and sends the next flight from where and when the ball met the paddle. The first two to press Play take the sides; whoever comes later watches.
