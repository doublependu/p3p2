This page stands in for a game where one page runs things for everybody. You move your own figure, and say where it is in your presence. The host's page runs the bots and the ball, and ten times a second tells each player, in one message, only what that player can see. The bots go only for players who are here: a player whose page is in the background is nobody's target. The round is a value the room keeps, which the host writes: whoever comes into a running round starts from it, and when the host goes, the next carries on from what it last saw.
