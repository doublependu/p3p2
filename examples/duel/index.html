    This page stands in for a game in turns with something hidden. Two take the seats, and each hides five squares on their own board. The squares stay in that player's browser: the room's log gets only a promise about them, a hash of the squares and a salt. Pick a square on the other board, and pick it again to probe it; the other player's page answers by itself, whenever it is open, today or another day. At the end both reveal their squares, and every page checks them against the promise and against every answer. Anybody else in the room watches, and sees nothing hidden until the end. No bundler and no modules: the client comes from one script tag.
